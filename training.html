WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

Learn the skills that care work demands.

The programme can cover foundational caregiving knowledge, practical skills, communication, ethics, safeguarding and professional practice.

01Caregiving Foundations

Understand the role, responsibilities, qualities and professional boundaries of a caregiver.

02Practical Care Skills

Develop practical approaches to assisting with everyday care and support.

03Communication

Build respectful communication skills for working with clients, families and care teams.

04Safeguarding & Ethics

Explore dignity, confidentiality, professional conduct and safeguarding principles.

05Healthcare Awareness

Develop appropriate awareness of health, safety and the caregiver’s role within a care team.

06Practical Experience

Programme components can include assessments and practical placement opportunities where applicable.
