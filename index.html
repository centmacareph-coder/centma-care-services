What services does Centma provide?

Centma provides caregiving and domiciliary home care, caregiving training, housekeeping and professional cleaning services.

Can I arrange care for a loved one in Nigeria?

Yes. Contact our team with the person's location and the kind of support needed so we can discuss the appropriate service.

Can I apply for caregiving training online?

Yes. Use the enquiry form below or WhatsApp us to begin your enrolment process and receive the current training information.

Do you offer services outside Port Harcourt?

Centma has service and training availability in Port Harcourt, Lagos, Benin and Warri. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific location.

How do I request a quote?

Send us your details through the form below or WhatsApp. Tell us the service you need, your location and any important requirements.
