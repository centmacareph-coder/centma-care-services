FAQ

Helpful answers before you begin.

If your question is not covered, contact us directly and our team can assist.

What services does Centma provide?

Centma provides caregiving and home care, caregiving training, housekeeping and professional cleaning services.

How do I request home care?

Use the Request a Service page to send your details, or contact Centma through WhatsApp or phone.

Can families in the diaspora arrange care in Nigeria?

Yes. Families can contact Centma to discuss support needs for loved ones in Nigeria.

How do I register for caregiving training?

Use the online Training Registration page or contact Centma directly for enrolment information.

Where is Centma located?

The Port Harcourt office is on Old Aba Road, Opposite Customary Court, Rumuogba, Port Harcourt. Centma also lists service or training availability in Lagos, Benin and Warri.

Can I speak to someone before booking?

Yes. You can call 0916 000 6043, email Centma, or chat on WhatsApp.
